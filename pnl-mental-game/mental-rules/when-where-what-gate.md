# WHEN / WHERE / WHAT Gate

## Purpose

The goal is not to eliminate emotion before trading.

Emotion may still be high.

The goal is to stop emotion from becoming the decision-maker.

## Use This Gate Before

- every new order
- every re-entry
- every direction flip
- every pair switch
- every chase attempt

## WHEN

Am I inside the permitted decision window?

If not, NO TRADE.

## WHERE

Am I at or very close to a meaningful taught strike reference?

If not, NO TRADE.

## WHAT

Is the actual red-car BUY or SELL template present?

If not, NO TRADE.

## Final Rule

All three must pass:

WHEN
-> WHERE
-> WHAT

If any one fails, or cannot be answered clearly:

NO TRADE.

Emotion can be high.

Process still has authority.
