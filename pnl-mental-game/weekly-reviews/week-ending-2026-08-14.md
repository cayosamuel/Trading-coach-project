## First Process Break

The review should focus on the first point where the process left the original recognition decision.

Typical chain:

1. Recognize setup
2. Enter
3. Small opposing movement appears
4. Confidence drops
5. Exit / flip
6. Frustration increases
7. Re-enter or switch market
8. Original thesis later reappears

The first confidence break matters more than counting the total number of later trades.

## Carry-Forward Rules

1. One recognition decision = one directional thesis.
2. Multiple tickets are acceptable only when they express the same planned thesis.
3. Do not flip direction because of small opposing movement unless the taught template is actually invalidated.
4. Do not pair-hop merely because confidence drops.
5. Before every new order, re-entry, flip, or pair switch, ask:
   - WHEN?
   - WHERE?
   - WHAT?
6. A losing week does not automatically mean recognition was wrong.
7. Grade recognition and execution separately.
